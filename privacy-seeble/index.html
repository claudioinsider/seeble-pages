# Privacy Policy — Seeble **Last updated**: November 25, 2026 **App**: Seeble **Developer**: Insider Hub LLC **Contact**: support@appseeble.com --- ## 1. Introduction Seeble ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Seeble mobile application. By using Seeble, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. --- ## 2. Information We Collect ### 2.1 Information You Provide - **Email address** — used for account creation and authentication - **Name** — used for your profile and personalization - **Phone number** — used for your profile (optional) - **Profile photo** — uploaded by you to personalize your account ### 2.2 Information We Process During App Use - **Palm image** — captured locally on your device when using the Palm Reading feature. This image is processed temporarily to generate your reading and is NOT stored on our servers. - **Chat messages** — messages you send in the Chat feature are sent to our servers so our team can prepare a response. Messages are not stored permanently beyond the current session. - **Astrological preferences** — your zodiac sign, birth date, and compatibility preferences used to prepare your readings. - **Soul mate preferences** — gender, age range, and ethnic background preferences used to select your soul mate image. ### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information - **App usage data** — which features you use, to improve the app experience. - **Device information** — operating system version and device type, for compatibility purposes. - **Advertising and device identifiers** — on iOS, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and only if you allow it through the App Tracking Transparency prompt the app shows you; on Android, the Google Advertising ID. Our attribution partner also creates its own identifier for your app installation. - **Marketing attribution data** — which ad or campaign your installation came from, and the in-app milestones listed in section 3 (creating an account, starting a free trial, subscribing, buying credits), including the amount and currency of a purchase. ### 2.4 Apple Health (HealthKit) If you turn on Apple Health sync (off by default), Seeble writes the duration of your completed meditation, breathing, and Flow sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. This is **write-only** — the app does not read any data from Apple Health — and your health data **stays on your device**; it is never sent to our servers or shared with any third party. You can turn the sync off at any time in your profile. --- ## 3. How We Use Your Information | Data | Purpose | |------|---------| | Email, Name, Phone | Account management and authentication | | Profile photo | Display in your profile | | Palm image | Produce your palm reading (temporary, not stored) | | Chat messages | Respond to your messages | | Astrological data | Produce personalized horoscopes and readings | | Soul mate preferences | Select your cosmic soul mate image | | Advertising identifier | Measure which ads bring people to Seeble | | Purchase amount and currency | Measure how well our ads perform | --- ## 4. Third-Party Services We use the following third-party services to operate the app: ### 4.1 Supabase We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our backend database and storage provider. Your profile data (email, name, phone, profile photo) is stored securely on Supabase servers. - **Supabase Privacy Policy**: https://supabase.com/privacy ### 4.2 Cloud processing providers We rely on commercial cloud-processing providers to deliver chat responses and palm-reading content within reasonable response times. These providers receive only the data strictly required to fulfill your request (message text or palm image) and do not retain it for their own purposes. ### 4.3 AppsFlyer We use AppsFlyer Ltd. (appsflyer.com) as our marketing attribution partner, to measure which ads bring people to Seeble. AppsFlyer receives a hashed account identifier — **never your email address** — your advertising identifier when you allow it, and the value and currency of your purchases. AppsFlyer shares the attribution result with the advertising networks we run campaigns on, currently Meta Platforms, Inc. and Google LLC. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before AppsFlyer starts measuring and passes your choice to AppsFlyer, which passes it on to Google. If you decline, AppsFlyer still counts the installation, but tells the advertising networks not to use your data for ad measurement or personalization. - **AppsFlyer Privacy Policy**: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy/ - **Meta Privacy Policy**: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/ - **Google Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy --- ## 5. Data Storage and Security - Your profile data is stored on Supabase servers with encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS). - Palm reading images are processed temporarily and are NOT stored on any server. - Chat messages are processed in real time and are not permanently stored by us. - We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. --- ## 6.
